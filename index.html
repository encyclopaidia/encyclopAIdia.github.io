What we're building

EncyclopAIdia is a resource for tracking and learning about key terms and topics in AI. Our goal is to build a shared vocabulary for decision makers and policy makers.

First release on the way

We're putting the finishing touches on the first version of two resources: the AI Glossary and the AI Guide. Keep an eye out for our release.

We’ll only email you when the first release is ready.
